Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026
Android application: com.smartPlay.OnMax

This Privacy Policy explains how OnMax (the “app”) handles information when you use its Android video player. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of OnMax. This policy covers the current version of the app, including local playback, video import and export, library organization, network playback and the in-app browser.

OnMax does not require an account, does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases, and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The current app has no active developer-operated backend for account login, content recommendations or uploading your library.

1. Information stored on your device

OnMax accesses and stores the following information on your device to provide the features you choose to use:

  • Selected videos and media information. Files you import, local copies, file names and paths, thumbnails, duration, file size and other playback information are used to display your library and play videos.
  • Library and playback records. Favorites, folders, saved video links, recent playback and playback progress help you organize videos and resume watching.
  • Search history and preferences. Search entries and settings such as playback speed, looping and resume playback are stored locally.
  • Technical state. The app creates a random installation identifier and local session information. It may read the device time zone and input-method identifier for local diagnostics; this does not include the text you type. Diagnostic information may appear in device logs. The current version does not send these records to an analytics or developer-operated server.

We do not automatically upload your local video files, library, viewing history or search history to our servers. Network activity that you initiate is described below.

2. Network playback, search and third-party websites

When you open a network video, load a remote image, save supported online media or visit a website in the app, your device connects to the relevant content provider. To deliver that content, the provider receives your IP address, the requested URL and standard connection information. Depending on the request and website, this may also include browser or player information, request headers, cookies and information you submit to that website.

When you submit a search phrase instead of a web address, OnMax opens Google Search with that phrase. Google receives the search terms and associated connection information. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Websites opened in the in-app browser may use cookies, browser storage, analytics or advertising under their own policies. OnMax's own interface is ad-free, but third-party pages may contain advertising. We do not control those websites or their retention practices. Review a website's privacy policy before signing in or providing personal information.

Online playback or caching can continue to make requests while that activity is running. Local playback of a fully imported video does not require a connection to a content provider.

3. Permissions and device access

  • Files and media: OnMax uses Android's file or media picker to access videos you select. On older Android versions, storage permission may be needed to read or save video files. Exported videos are saved to device storage.
  • Internet and network status: These support web browsing, network playback, remote content and checking whether a network connection is available.
  • Background processing: Android background-work and wake-lock capabilities support media caching and related work. These capabilities do not give us access to your contacts or location.
  • Camera and microphone: The current Android package declares camera and microphone permissions, but this release does not provide a recording feature or request these permissions for importing and playing videos. You can leave them denied.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings under Apps → OnMax → Permissions. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working. OnMax does not request access to your contacts or precise location.

4. Use and sharing of information

On-device information is used to operate the player, organize your library, remember your choices and diagnose problems locally. We do not sell your personal information or share your library with advertisers.

Information leaves your device when you choose an online feature, interact with a third-party website or contact us. The relevant content providers, websites and communication providers process that information to carry out your request. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Please do not send videos or other sensitive information unless necessary for your inquiry.

When you visit this privacy policy page, its hosting providers receive standard web request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to serve and protect the page. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms.

We may disclose information we actually hold if required by applicable law or a valid legal request. This does not give us access to videos or records that remain only on your device.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local app records remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior.

  • You can remove saved entries and clear search history using the available controls in OnMax. Removing a library entry does not necessarily erase the original video or every related local file.
  • To remove app-managed records, cached content and browser storage together, use Android Settings → Apps → OnMax → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. The exact labels vary by device. This also resets your preferences.
  • Original media and videos exported to shared device storage may remain after app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Delete those files separately with your gallery or file manager.
  • Android or your device provider may back up or restore app data depending on your device settings. Review and manage those backups separately through that provider.

There is no OnMax account to delete. We cannot remotely erase files held only on your device. If you contact us, we retain correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you have sent us using the privacy contact below. Third-party websites control the deletion of information they hold.

6. Security

OnMax uses Android's application storage protections, and stores its random installation identifier using secure storage. Exported or otherwise shared files are subject to your device's storage permissions and may be accessible to other authorized apps.

HTTPS connections use transport encryption when supported by the service you access. Protection also depends on the source URL, website and your device; do not assume that every user-supplied media connection is encrypted. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

OnMax does not ask users to create profiles or provide their age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so that we can address the request and delete information we hold where appropriate. Parents and guardians should supervise access to third-party websites and online media.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing a new use of personal information.
